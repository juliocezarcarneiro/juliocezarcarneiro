About

I'm Julio Carneiro, a data analyst in Toronto focused on hospitality: restaurants, cafés and hotels.

Before analytics I spent five years running kitchen operations. I used sales, inventory and waste data to control costs, and cut food waste from 8% to 2%. That's still how I work: start from the business question, find the number that matters, and recommend what to fix first.

What I work on

  • Weekly KPI reports a manager can read in five minutes
  • Food cost and margin analysis by location, category and menu item
  • Labour cost against sales, by day and shift
  • Dashboards in Power BI, Tableau or Excel
