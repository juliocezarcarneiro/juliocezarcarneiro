Before I worked with data, I ran kitchen operations for five years. Every week I looked at sales, inventory and waste, and every week the same questions came back: what did we throw away, what did it cost, and which items actually made money.

Using that data, we cut food waste from 8% to 2%. Nothing fancy: a consistent weekly count, a simple spreadsheet, and a habit of looking at it.

That is the idea behind this site. Most restaurants, cafés and hotels already collect the data they need in their POS, payroll and supplier invoices. What is missing is the time to turn it into a short answer.

## What I'll post here

- **Notes:** practical SQL, Python, Excel and Power BI techniques, using hospitality examples like menu items, shifts and covers.
- **Projects:** case studies with a business question and the number that answered it.
- **Resources:** free books and courses worth your time.
