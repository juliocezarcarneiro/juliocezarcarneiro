Hi, I'm Julio Carneiro

I'm a data analyst focused on hospitality. I help restaurants, cafés and hotels see where their money goes: food cost, labour, margins and guest data, turned into decisions a manager can act on this week.

Before analytics I ran kitchen operations for five years and cut food waste from 8% to 2%. Here I write notes on data, share projects, and collect free resources. More about me.
